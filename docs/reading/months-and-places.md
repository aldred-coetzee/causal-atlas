# Where are open series by month, or below the country?

A reader's page for the brief `docs/specs/2026-11-11-months-and-places.md`. Read on 2026-11-11. Every access or licence claim below was read by the reader on the page whose address sits beside it, in this session. Nothing was downloaded. Three small checks touched a server beyond a page, and are named where they are used: a one-byte request to see whether a file is served without a login (HFD, HMD), a header-only request (Ember, FluNet), and two count queries to the USGS API, which return one number each. "Not confirmed" means what it says, and such a source is not ranked. Bias lines are the reader's notes drawn from what the pages say; they are not quotes.

## Checked and not usable

- **Human Mortality Database, weekly deaths (STMF)**: licence is CC BY 4.0 (<https://www.mortality.org/Data/UserAgreement>), but a request for `stmf.csv` was redirected to `/Account/Login`. Needs an account. Eurostat (row 6) and HFD (row 11) cover part of it.
- **EOG VIIRS night lights** (Colorado School of Mines): "Users will need to register a free account with verified e-mail address to access EOG's resources", and from June 2026 programmatic access is for paid subscribers (<https://eogdata.mines.edu/products/register/>). Row 2 replaces it.
- **GRDC data portal**: download by a form that sends a link by email; data "released only for research purposes", and redistribution is not allowed (<https://grdc.bafg.de/data/data_portal/>). Row 8 is the open route to part of it.
- **WHO FluNet**: the CSV is open (<https://www.who.int/tools/flunet>), but WHO's data policy shares Member State data "on terms that allow non-commercial, not-for-profit use of the Data for public health purposes" (<https://www.who.int/about/policies/publishing/data-policy>). Non-commercial. WHO's dataset-specific terms page was not read.
- **IMF data**: "For any potential commercial reuse of IMF Data", permission must be requested by email (<https://www.imf.org/en/about/copyright-and-terms>). Commercial use needs permission, so not usable for an MIT project.
- **Global Data Lab (sub-national HDI and others)**: "Commercial use is only possible after written permission of the Global Data Lab." (<https://globaldatalab.org/termsofuse/>).

## Not confirmed (not ranked)

- **Ember monthly electricity**: direct CSV, no account (<https://ember-energy.org/data/monthly-electricity-data/>; the file answered 200 to a header request), "released under a Creative Commons Attribution Licence (CC-BY-4.0)", 88 areas. The **start month is not stated**; the methodology's "215 countries from 2000" is about the yearly data. Whether most areas have 15 years by month is unknown.
- **ILOSTAT**: CC BY 4.0 since 3 May 2023 (<https://www.ilo.org/node/9321>); the ILO's own R client fetches `https://rplumber.ilo.org/files/...` with no key (<https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ilostat/Rilostat/master/R/get_ilostat.R>). The bulk page answered 403, and **which monthly series exist, and for how many countries, was not read**.
- **JRC ASAP**: GAUL 1 regions, every ten days, vegetation (FPAR), rainfall, temperature, soil moisture; "Reuse is authorised, provided the source is acknowledged." (<https://agricultural-production-hotspots.ec.europa.eu/download.php>). The **period of the regional statistics is not stated** ("complete time series"), they come one country and indicator at a time from an on-page tool, and the GAUL boundary terms were not read. Promising and European; worth one more look.
- **IBTrACS tropical cyclones**: no account needed, 1840s storms onward in all basins (<https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/international-best-track-archive>); the page gives only "full and open access" under the WMO data policy and points commercial users to WMO Resolution 40. **No licence sentence found**; the metadata page timed out twice.
- **JODI Oil**: yearly CSVs of monthly data, 2002 to 2026, free download (<https://www.jodidata.org/oil/database/data-downloads.aspx>). **No licence or terms found**; the API needs an account.
- **Eurostat EU27 trade by partner, monthly** (`ext_st_eu27_2020sitc`): 2002-01 to 2026-08, 6,397,337 observations, a partner dimension (<https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/api/dissemination/sdmx/2.1/dataflow/ESTAT/ext_st_eu27_2020sitc>). Partner count not read, and the copyright notice's exception for data on non-EU countries may make the partner rows non-commercial.
- **UNHCR refugee statistics API**: yearly by country of origin and asylum (<https://api.unhcr.org/docs/refugee-statistics.html>); key and licence not seen in the part read; other pages answered 403.
- **IDMC**: the API documentation page read did not state its terms; a search result says a key is issued by email, which would rule it out.
- **UN Comtrade**: the licence page only redirected; search results say the full API needs a subscription key.

No source was confirmed for **transport** or **elections**; neither was looked for beyond the sources above.

## The three to fetch first

1. **FAOSTAT Consumer Price Indices and exchange rates, monthly.** About 3 MB, the same host, licence and file shape as FAOSTAT crops already held, so it is one adapter away. It turns prices and money from yearly (WDI) into about 310 months a country.
2. **OpenDengue, highest temporal extract.** Small, CC BY or CC BY-SA, a domain the table lacks entirely (disease), weekly or monthly for 102 countries and below the country in 40, and in the places where the held outcomes sit.
3. **ACAG SatPM2.5, monthly at 0.1 degree.** A new domain on a grid that aggregates straight onto the project's 0.5 degree cells, global, monthly for about 26 years: the most places times points of any source here that is not daily. Read its size and the bucket's version before fetching.

Fourth, if a yearly one is wanted: the night lights (row 2), about 1 GB, every land cell for 33 years.

## Checked again by the project session, 2026-11-11

A helper's page is a claim until some of it is read a second time. Read again at source, for the first of the three:

- FAOSTAT's own list of bulk files names both files at the addresses in row 9, with 266,292 and 151,212 rows, updated 2026-10-20 and 2026-09-16.
- FAO's terms say what row 9 quotes.
- The consumer prices metadata, which the reader could not open, was read as text and searched: its first sources are the IMF's consumer price indices, with gaps filled from UNSD, OECD, BCEAO, ECCB, UNCTAD and national offices, and it holds no sentence on licence, reuse or consent. So FAO passes on, under CC BY 4.0, figures whose first maker (the IMF, in "Checked and not usable" above) asks permission for commercial reuse of its own releases. Taken as usable under FAO's terms, with that written beside it in `docs/data-sources.md`; the files stay on the box, as all data does.
- OpenDengue's repository has no licence file at its root, so the difference between its site (CC BY 4.0) and its paper (CC BY-SA) is not settled there. Not fetched until it is.

Nothing else on the page was read a second time.
