# Review of the work so far, and a proposed way forward

Written 2026-11-09 at the owner's request ("Review what we have done and propose best way forward"). A proposal: nothing here is started until he has answered. Figures are from the results files named; nothing new was computed.

## Where we are

Four days, about 8.2M of 20M tokens, 39 decisions, 12 results files. No method has been run on real data and no real relationship has been found or tested. Every test so far ran on worlds we built ourselves, with one invented shape of link planted in them.

## What is solid

- **A data base.** The Africa grid, conflict events, drought and El Niño are fetched, licensed and logged (`results/1a-data-check.md`, `docs/data-sources.md`).
- **A baseline that behaves** after its blow-ups were fixed (`results/r1-twin.md`).
- **A quiet gate.** After the fix, worlds with no link raise a lead in 0 of 15 at both lengths in the last run (`results/framework-curve.md`).
- **A harness with checks on itself:** nulls, a ceiling, a floor, a truth check, a seed ledger.
- **A ruler** (`results/framework-curve.md`), with three facts:
  1. Told exactly where a link is, the test hears one that causes about 4% of events, 15 of 15 at 240 months. Searching blind among 1,280 columns it hears a link only when it causes most of the events (8 of 15 at a 63% share). Being told is more than ten times as sensitive as searching.
  2. Length of record matters more than any choice of method: at 96 months blind search works at no strength.
  3. Width costs about one step of strength. But most of the loss is already there with only the two true inputs searched (6 of 15 at a 4% share against 15 told exactly).

## What went wrong

1. **The hardest ground came first.** Conflict events are rare and clumped, the record that can be scored is 20 years, and a place's own history already predicts well. The plan's own planted strengths were 1.05, 1.15 and 1.40. At 1.40 even the test told the exact link hears it in 1 of 15 at 96 months and 5 of 15 at 240. So the design asked blind search for something the data could not carry, and nobody had measured that before setting marks.
2. **Everything was tested against our own invention.** One link shape, independent decoys, strengths not tied to any real effect. Four methods were tuned or judged against it. Whether real links resemble it in size or shape is unknown.
3. **Each miss led to another instrument on the same question.** First detector, refit forecaster, screen, shortlist. The framework proposal named this. The detection curve was the first step that measured what is possible, and it answered more in one run than the four methods did.
4. **Process cost.** Writing the rule first, nulls and unseen months caught real errors (a gate that fired on noise, a cap that deafened the screen) and stay. The volume of decisions, audits and re-marking around them was heavy for what it produced.

## Proposed way forward

**Keep the aim. Change the proving ground: go to real data with known answers now, on data already held, and make the ruler part of what the project reports.**

1. **One reading, one page: how large are real links?** The step the curve's rule already names for this outcome. Published sizes of climate to conflict links, set against the curve's size column. Expectation, from memory and unchecked: they are far smaller than blind search can hear on this design. About 150k tokens.
2. **A real known-answer test on data we already hold: El Niño to drought across Africa.** Both series are on the box and overlap for about 75 years, against the 20 the conflict record allows. The accepted answer, from memory and to be confirmed at source before the rule is written: El Niño years are drier in southern Africa and wetter in parts of East Africa, at lags of some months. El Niño is hidden among decoys and the search must name it, with where and at what lag. No new data, no new domain. About 1M to 2M tokens; my estimates have run low.
   - If it fails with 75 years and a link science accepts, the search design is wrong and is rebuilt before anything else.
   - If it passes, it is the project's first true result on real data, and the base to add one data set at a time (vegetation, then prices), each with a known link to rediscover first. New data sets need the owner's yes ("Not now: more domains").
3. **Report the limit beside every answer.** For any pair of data sets: the links found, and the smallest link that would have been heard. "Nothing found" becomes "nothing causing more than X% of events", which is honest and useful.
4. **Two modes, said plainly.** Blind search hears loud links. A named idea is tested about ten times more sensitively. Both are in the owner's words of 2026-11-05 (decision 0021).
5. **Conflict comes back last, with the ruler in hand,** and probably at a coarser scale (regions and quarters) where there is more to hear per unit.

**Parked:** more methods on the synthetic conflict world; the mark on seeds 7101 to 7140; the combinations rung. If one link cannot be heard blind, a combination, which is a far wider search, cannot either on the same record. This is the part of the aim I think is out of reach for conflict with 20 years of data, and it should be said now.

## The other options

- **Keep improving the search on the synthetic conflict world** (fewer columns, a better statistic). There are real gains to be had, since most of the loss is in choosing among a true input's own columns. Not recommended now: it tunes further to our own invention.
- **Stop here and write it up.** The curve is a clean negative result: how strong a link must be to be found blind in 20 years of gridded conflict data. Fair, but step 2 is cheap beside what has been spent and tells whether the engine is any use on real data.

## What this changes in the record

Stage 3 of `docs/framework-proposal.md` (real data with known answers) would come before Stage 2 (related inputs and combinations). The two questions still open with the owner, the ladder's reach and whether Stage 3's new data is allowed, are answered in part by this: step 2 uses only held data, and anything new is asked for one data set at a time.
