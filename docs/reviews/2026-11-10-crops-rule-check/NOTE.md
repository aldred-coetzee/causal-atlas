# About this folder

An independent reader checked the rule for the other crops and drought (`results/crops-drought.md`), its code and its made-up results before any real yield of those crops was set beside drought. It read the repo at commit 3436703 with no part in the design, under the brief in `SPEC.md`, committed before it started, with one hard rule: no real yield of the 47 other crops beside any real drought value.

- `report.md` is the reader's report. It came back as a message, because the reader could not write the file itself, and was saved here unchanged by the project session. Its verdict: the real record may be read after five changes of wording; no condition of the rule changes.
- The scripts and logs are the reader's own and ran against commit 3436703, except as listed below. `scripts/panel_x.py` is its copy of the made-up record generator with extra settings; `logs/runs.jsonl` holds its runs, one line a world. Its seeds are in `results/seed-ledger.md`.
- **Changed by the project session before committing:** absolute paths in `shape.py`, `shared.py`, `interval.py`, `show.py` and `batch.sh` were replaced by paths from the repo root, each marked in the file. `shared.py` was run again afterwards and gives its log exactly. Nothing else in the reader's files was altered.
- **Written by the project session after the report, not by the reader**, each saying so in its first line:
  - `scripts/followup.py`, `logs/followup.jsonl`: the reader's hardest world (a curving drift shared by drought and yields at 2.5 times the registered strength, no link in the other crops) on eight more seeds with all 62 shifts.
  - `scripts/curve.py`, `logs/curve.log`: the size of the slow curve in each country's drought series, alone, on the real panel and on fifteen made-up worlds. No yield is read.
- What was changed in response: the section "Independent check" in `results/crops-drought.md`, the README paragraph, and `docs/decisions/0058-what-the-independent-check-changed-for-other-crops.md`.
- The project session checked these of its claims against the counted rows and its own logs before acting: the best no-link gain at mid-year (0.78%, seed 25001); that the capped and uncapped gains are equal in 641 of 720 registered readings; that across the 65 runs with only the cereal-like crops linked the true years beat no shift in 16 and 60 or more in 15; the row for seed 26011; that its scripts load real yields only; that nothing outside this folder was changed. One slip in the report: it says 33 seeds and lists 24; 24 is what its scripts and logs use.
