- `SPEC.md` is its brief, committed before it reported (commit 6d09c63).

- The scripts and logs are the reader's own and ran against commit a1beda9. Its seeds, 24001 to 24008, are in `results/seed-ledger.md`.
- What was changed in response is `docs/decisions/0053-what-the-independent-check-changed-in-the-harvest-result.md` and the result sections of `results/harvest-drought.md`.
- The project session reproduced these of its numbers with the project's own run before acting (seed 24050 for the resampling): 0.84% as a share of typical error; ten countries 55% and twenty 85% of the gain; 28 of 141 countries to bring the summed gain to zero; the interval with countries and years both resampled, 0.61% to 2.78%; the chosen third 5.32%, the other ranked records −0.21%, the unranked 2.31%. All agree with the report.
- Everything here is a check on a record already scored once. Nothing in this folder is a second result.
