# 0014: A gate that passes does not stop

**Date:** 2026-11-05. **Status:** accepted. Changes rule 6 of the brief, at the owner's word.

## Context

The owner said *"don't ask me again"*. Rule 6 of his brief requires that a request to stop pausing be
quoted back and confirmed, and it was. His answer: *"if in your mind the gate passes, then do not stop
to ask me. If you think it actually really requires guidance, then stop and ask me."*

## Decision

At each gate this session still writes the report of at most ten lines, with tokens against the cap,
and sends it. Then:

- **It carries on, without waiting,** if the step met its pre-registered pass marks and nothing below
  applies.
- **It stops and asks** if a pass mark is missed, or a step is heading 25% over its estimate, or a cap
  would be passed; or a result is surprising enough to question the design; or a pre-registered rule
  would have to change in a way that could favour a result; or the choice is outside the Phase 1 plan.

This session's own judgement decides whether a gate passes, so each report says why it passed.

## What this does not change

Caps, the 25% rule, pre-registration, scoring the held-out set once, and next-pattern's two audits all
stand. Step 1d's end is always a stop: extending, stopping or changing method is the owner's decision,
and so is anything in "Not now".

## Consequences

If 1b and 1c pass, the held-out set is scored without a further yes from him. That cannot be undone,
which is why next-pattern's audit of the result and the independent review remain. A wrong call by this
session is now caught later, not at the gate, so reports must not soften a marginal pass.
