# 0012: A 2% margin and a 3-month lead

**Date:** 2026-11-05. **Status:** accepted.

## Context

The plan left these two open for the owner. His answer: *"Yes to margin and lead"*.

## Decision

The primary forecast is three months ahead, and a finding needs at least 2% lower Poisson
deviance than the baseline, as `docs/plan-phase-1.md` states with its other two criteria.

## Consequences

Both are now part of the pre-registered rule, set before any data was fetched. The 2% is
judgement, not derived from data; step 1b shows what size of real effect it corresponds to.
This does not start step 1a, which waits for his separate yes.
