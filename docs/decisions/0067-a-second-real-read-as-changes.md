# 0067: A second read of the yearly panel, every series as its change from the year before

Status: accepted, 2026-11-12. Made by this session under decision 0040; reported to the owner, his to overrule.

## Context

The first read across the real table was silent (`results/stage-b-first-read.md`), and its planted runs showed the read could not hear a steady link of up to 35% of an outcome's year-to-year movement on this table. On made-up tables shaped like it, the cause is the outcome keeping its level, so that its own past takes most of what a link adds (`docs/reading/series-that-keep-their-level.md`). Reading every series as its change from the year before hears such links; counted once on 300 made-up tables, every mark held (`results/change-count.md`).

Reading the same real years a second time is a choice that could favour a result: two reads have two chances to pass something by chance, and 1996 to 2014 are no longer untouched.

## Decision

1. **The yearly panel is read a second time as changes**, under `results/stage-c-second-read.md`, committed before any run. Everything but the change is the first read's, unchanged: panel, ties, groups, finders, steward, referee, years, the rule for the last ten years.
2. **A pass in this read is a candidate, not a result.** Only a candidate confirmed on 2015 to 2024, which no read has opened, is called a lead.
3. **The placebo and planted runs are made again on the changed table** with fresh seeds, the same marks, and twelve planted runs in place of six so that a silence can be read against more links.
4. **Rearrangement in years is done after the change**, so no change is taken across a seam; links are planted in the values before the change, as in the count on made-up tables.
5. **An independent reader reads rule and code before the first run**, as for the first read.

## Considered and not done

- **Reading both ways under one bar** (eighty rows). The first read is already made and cannot be made again; a joint bar would need it to be.
- **Changing the years** so the scored years were ones never read. The only unread years are the last ten, and they are the confirmation. Using them for the read would leave nothing to confirm on.
- **Each series less its own baseline** in place of the change. The same hearing on made-up tables, more machinery, and it takes something from the referee.
- **Tightening the placebo marks with more runs.** As decision 0065: the untouched years are the second guard.

## Consequences

After this read 1996 to 2014 of the panel are read twice, once each way, and the spent ledger says so. A third way of reading these years would need a reason of the same kind, found on made-up tables first.
