# 0042: The confirmation passed; an outlook is published from the model as tested, and its later scoring is registered now

Status: accepted, 2026-11-09. Made by this session under decision 0040; reported to the owner, his to overrule.

## Context

The forecast check of decision 0041 passed on four continental fields never read before (`results/real-enso-continents.md`, "Results, step 2"): pooled S +0.171, interval +0.120 to +0.220, beyond all 24 shifted controls and every baseline alone. The held El Niño index runs to the value centred on 2026-09 and shows a strong event under way. The drought file ends at 2024-12.

## Decision

- An outlook for the months to 2027-09 is published under its own rule, `results/outlook-2026-27.md`, written and committed before it is computed.
- The headline is the model exactly as it was tested (fitted on 1951 to 1999). A refit on all years is shown beside it and is called untested.
- Africa is left out: its confirmation missed. Europe is left out: no claim.
- The terms for 2025-01 to 2027-09 are committed now and their scoring against a later release of the drought file is fixed now, in the same rule.

## Why each choice could favour a result, and the guard

- Choosing between the tested model and a refit after seeing both could pick the more striking map. The choice is fixed before either is computed.
- A forecast published without a scoring rule can be read as right whatever happens. The rule, the size that counts as a signal, and what counts as a hit are fixed now.
- The table of what past terms were followed by is drawn from years already scored. It is labelled as description, with its bands fixed before it is computed.

## Consequences

- The project has a public, dated forecast that can fail.
- An outlook is not on the "Not now" list, but it is a product a reader could act on. It carries a plain statement that it is a method test and not a warning service.
