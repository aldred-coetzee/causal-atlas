# 0010: Three Phase 1 design choices delegated and taken

**Date:** 2026-11-05. **Status:** accepted, subject to next-pattern's audit of the plan.

## Context

The Phase 1 plan put design choices to the owner. His answer: *"Not sure what margin
and leads is, leave other decisions to you and controlling session"*.

## Decision

As written in `docs/plan-phase-1.md`, the project session takes three choices, and
next-pattern may challenge them in its audit:

- Food prices are tested separately from climate, on the cells and months that have
  them, because a price series may exist only because a crisis started.
- Three inputs, not four.
- Synthetic panels run at full size unless that exceeds 48 hours; then one replicate per
  panel runs at full size and the rest on 2,000 cells, disclosed.

The 2% margin and the 3-month lead are not covered by this and remain open for the owner.

## Consequences

The delegation covers these design choices only. It is not read as a request to stop
pausing: every gate still waits for the owner's yes (rule 6), including the start of step
1a. Should he mean more than this, rule 6 is quoted back and he is asked to confirm.
