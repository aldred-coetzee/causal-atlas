# 0011: Changes to the Phase 1 plan from next-pattern's audit

**Date:** 2026-11-05. **Status:** accepted. Supersedes the 48-hour synthetic panel fallback in
decision 0010; the rest of 0010 stands.

## Context

next-pattern audited the plan at a236e32 and decided these under the owner's delegation (*"leave
other decisions to you and controlling session"*). All are edits to the pre-registered rule, made
before any data was fetched. The audit is held in the owner's private configuration repo.

## Decision

- **Shuffle.** Year permutation destroys persistence, so it can flatter a persistent input. A circular
  shift by whole years is named as a second scheme, and one is fixed at the 1b gate.
- **Interval.** Nulls must hold large multi-year regional episodes, and inputs real-like persistence.
  1d reports the share of the gain owed to the largest country and the largest year.
- **1b pass mark.** At most 1 false positive of 20 per null, with the exact binomial bound.
- **Generator.** One Sonnet subagent writes `synth/` from a spec withholding the test models, about
  100k fresh. The spec and cost go to the owner at the 1b gate, and he can say no.
- **Prices.** A row with no price feature at t is dropped for both models and never passed as missing.
- **Small rules.** The interval is two-sided. Shuffled refits use frozen settings. M is also reported
  at B's settings.
- **PCMCI and convergent cross mapping are deferred until after the 1d verdict.** This is a deferral,
  not a drop, of the brief's candidate methods, and the owner can reverse it at any gate.
- **Runtime.** One full-size replicate per panel, the rest on 2,000 cells, 24-hour ceiling, disclosed
  as a departure from the brief's "same size".

This session added two points the audit left open. Both schemes also run on the planted panels, since
a scheme must be shown to detect as well as stay quiet. If both qualify, the circular shift is used.

## Consequences

The circular shift's smallest p is about 0.033 on a series of about 30 years, which cannot meet the
0.025 a Bonferroni correction would ask for. The verdict states this as a limit rather than hiding it.
The 1b estimate falls from 600k to 450k and the Phase 1 total from 1.9M to 1.75M. The margin, the lead
and the start of step 1a remain the owner's.
