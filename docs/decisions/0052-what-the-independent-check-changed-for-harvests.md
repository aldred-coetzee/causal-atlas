# 0052: What the independent check changed before any real harvest is read

Status: accepted, 2026-11-10. Made by this session under decision 0040; reported to the owner, his to overrule. Amends the rule in `results/harvest-drought.md`. No change makes a pass easier.

## Context

The rule required an independent reader before any real yield was set against drought. Its report is `docs/reviews/2026-11-10-harvest-rule-check/report.md`. It found no leak, reproduced the made-up results, and found the real path as written. Its verdict was that the scoring may go ahead after changes. Three findings bear on what a result may be said to mean:

1. **Drift.** Moving the driver by whole years keeps a slow trend where it was, so a drift shared by drought and yields beats the shifted years. On its own records with a shared drift and no link, the model's side met every condition in 6 of 37, with S up to 1.56%. The flat driver, which has a straight line for each country and calendar month removed, stopped all 37. Real country drought does drift: four countries in five dry over the record.
2. **The ruler is the best case.** The made-up test sees exactly the moisture that moves the yield. With a blurred series the 20% link was read 5 times in 8.
3. **A shared cause is read as a link** every time.

## Decision

- The size reported is the smaller of S and S with the flat driver. The reader offered this as the stricter of two options. The verdict already required both to be at least 0.2%, so no reading changes; what changes is that a drift cannot raise the size that is reported.
- The rule's wording is corrected on what a pass and a silence mean, and on four places where the words and the code differed. The code is the registered thing in each and is not changed.
- The real run reads both variants before writing either.
- No new marked panel and no re-run: nothing in the verdict changed, so the counted records stand as read.

## Consequences

- A pass can be reported at a smaller size than S, never a larger one.
- A silence is claimed only against a link of the ruler's size in the index as measured.
- Had the reader's drift finding been met by a new condition instead, fresh seeds would have been needed. It was not, because the flat driver already carries that guard and held on every record tried, here and in the reader's runs.
