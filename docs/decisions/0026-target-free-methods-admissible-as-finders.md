# 0026: Methods that search without a target are admissible as finders

**Date:** 2026-11-05, evening. **Status:** accepted, reversible by the owner. A ruling he delegated to next-pattern, relayed by it. Input to the plan revision.

## Context

Next-pattern asked him whether open-ended inquiry with no objective belongs inside this project. His words: "Yes on multi-omics, your call on no loss function".

## Decision

Next-pattern's call: the project's brief does not change. It keeps prediction against a baseline, pre-registration and the caps. A method with no target (the random matrix test, multi-block shared factors, persistent homology) may be a candidate in the revision. What it reports counts only after it finds planted structure on fake worlds, stays quiet where none was planted, and passes the search-wide gate. The open-ended inquiry without deliverables is not part of this project and nothing is started for it.

Its reason: no target is needed to find something; a test is needed to believe it. The gates have nothing to test an objective-free inquiry against, so admitting it would switch off the rules the brief was written to keep.

## One point of wording, put back to next-pattern

The relay also said "and, where it claims to help prediction, improves the forecast out of time". Read alone, that could let a report that claims no prediction count as a finding. The brief's rule 2 says "Nothing counts as a finding unless it beats that baseline out of time", and the ruling says the brief does not change. So, unless the owner rules otherwise: a target-free report that has passed the three tests above is a lead to be tested, and it becomes a finding only by beating the baseline out of time.

## Consequences

The revision sets the tests for any such candidate before it runs. `CLAUDE.md` is not edited on this relay. Nothing runs before the revision and his yes, and the order in decision 0023 is unchanged.
