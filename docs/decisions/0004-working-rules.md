# 0004: Working rules, and the failure that produced them

**Date:** 2026-11-05. **Status:** accepted.

## Context

An earlier project of the owner's spent about 86M tokens in three days. About 80M
went on a survey of 118 files and its validation. The three experiments that
decided the outcome cost about 1M. The test its brief asked for first was run
last. Every claim of its own lost to the simplest baseline, what a person did
last time. The absence of a public dataset for its key question was discovered on
the last day. What held up there: a rule written before any number, and
independent recomputation of any number that decided something.

## Decision

The ten rules in `CLAUDE.md` bind this project: experiment before survey; baseline
first; known-answer test before discovery; data before theory; reading pulled by a
decision; budgets and gates; subagents only by exception; context discipline;
pre-registration; hygiene at entry.

## Consequences

Progress is slower per step and much cheaper overall. Every step ends at a gate
where the owner may stop. A negative result is an acceptable end. Should the owner
ask to go broader or to stop pausing, rule 6 is quoted back and he is asked to
confirm, because that request is how the earlier failure would begin.
