# 0016: Broader within the rules, by the two sessions' joint plan

**Date:** 2026-11-05. **Status:** accepted. Replaces steps 4 and 5 of decision 0015 and adds to step 1.

## Context

After 0015, next-pattern put two options to the owner: broader within the rules, or broader without them, the
second needing his explicit confirmation because it would set aside rules 1, 5 and 6. His words, typed to
next-pattern and relayed by it here: *"for you and the project session to coordinate and communicate, be sure that
you do. Happy to go with your collective recommendations."* Rules 1, 5 and 6 therefore stand, as amended by 0014.
This is not a licence for open-ended collection or a methods survey.

## Decision

1. Finish 1b on the fixed generator, publishing rate ratio, share of cells and worth for each planted panel,
   inside the 450k estimate (stop at 560k).
2. Add one planted panel where the effect appears only when several inputs combine, and its matching null. This
   tests his multi-factor idea on synthetic data before any new source is fetched.
3. No 1c or 1d on real data. The held-out block stays unscored.
4. Add sources: at most five, 300k fresh for all of them, each named with the one-line mechanism it is there to
   test before it is fetched, each passing the 1a table (no login, licence, coverage, robots.txt read first), and
   entered in the manifest and `docs/data-sources.md`. Sonnet subagents for fetching stay within rule 7.
5. Then a pre-registered multi-input test as a revision of the Phase 1 plan, its margin set from what step 1 shows
   a plausible effect is worth. next-pattern checks the revision before it runs, as a short continuation of the
   plan audit; its result audit and the independent review stand. The held-out block is scored only when both
   sessions say the revision passes; if they disagree, it goes to the owner.
6. Method reading stays pulled: one question, one agent, one page, only when a named method fails a named test.

## Consequences

The 3M cap covers all of this. Reports go to the owner at each gate; he is stopped only where 0014 says. 0015's
"about 10 sources, 150k" no longer applies: fewer sources, each tied to a mechanism, at a larger cap.
