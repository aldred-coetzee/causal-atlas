# 0062: The finders and the steward: sixteen small members, a lift for each row, and a floor on what is sent

Status: accepted, 2026-11-11 (shaped 2026-11-10). Made by this session under decision 0040; reported to the owner, his to overrule.

## Context

Stage A1 counted the referee alone: told where a link is, it hears most kinds; told nothing of where, it heard 4 of 1,620 links (`results/finders-stage-a1.md`). So Stage A2 of `docs/plan-finders-and-referee.md` begins with a finder of where. Decision 0057 makes the finders a swarm of small plain-code members posting to one board, and the plan names five families. Every choice below was made on smoke worlds (seeds 47001 to 47999, never in a count). Each could favour a result: a finder that ranks well what was planted for it, or a steward that sends few rows and so lowers the bar, changes how much is heard and how much is passed falsely.

## Decision

1. **What a member sees.** The years before the referee's first scored year (35 of 60), each series in the referee's comparable form, and what each outcome's own history leaves in those years by the referee's own baselines, run on those years alone. Nothing from a scored year reaches the board; a test holds this.

2. **Where, by a scan.** For each term, the groups of five places are put in order of how far the term goes with the residual there, and the run from the top of at least five groups is taken that stands furthest above nothing (its correlation times the root of its places), either way up. All places when every group is taken.

3. **A lift for every row, never a test.** The whole search, scan included, is repeated with the outcome moved round to twenty other alignments (three years and more from the true one). A row's lift is how far it stands at the true alignment, in units of its own spread over the other alignments, above the best of the member's terms at a typical other alignment. So a member that chooses more (places, signs, series to gather) or tries more is not ranked higher for it; a test holds this. The referee alone says what holds.

4. **Sixteen members in five families.**
   - *Slope*: the plain line, one year and two years before.
   - *Shape*: a hinge above the input's own 80th and below its 20th percentile; a sign each place sets for itself; a sign that turns on which side of its own past a series common to all places stood.
   - *Extremes*: a tail event in the input (above its 90th, below its 10th percentile), alone and against the chance of a tail event in the outcome (four members).
   - *Pairs*: every pair as a product; and in a second round, the pairs among the series single-series members put forward, read from the board.
   - *Modes*: three main components of the block of series that differ by place, weights set with nothing of the outcome; and in a second round, the 5, 10 and 20 series highest on the plain line gathered with their signs, the choosing repeated at every other alignment.
   
   A member whose proposal names a second series (a pair, a sign that turns on a state) ranks what it adds beyond the plain lines through its parts, so that a series with no part in a link is not carried in on one that has. Hinge and tail members are plain: on smoke worlds, residualising them beyond the plain line cost threshold and extremes links (each form's scan chooses its own places, and a single-series form passes when its places are right).

5. **The steward.** A family's rows naming the same series for the same outcome count as one. Each family in turn sends its best, up to eight; what is left of forty goes to rows that most families arrived at separately, then by lift. Agreement decides the order, never the verdict.

6. **A floor of three on lift.** A row is sent only if its lift is at least three. With no floor every world sent forty rows and the bar stood at about 3.3 to 3.6; with it most worlds send 2 to 15 rows and the bar falls to about 2.3 to 2.7, because the bar is the largest chance departure among the rows sent. Fewer, better rows are heard more easily, and the bar still covers every row sent. The risk is the one the count measures: the bar is lower, so a row that is chance stands closer to it.

7. **Set aside from the plan.** Boosted trees (the plan's "trees and pairs"): on forty series and 35 early years they add a model fit per outcome per alignment with nothing a product and a hinge do not already propose, and their proposals would need a form the referee does not have. The lagged cross-spectrum between blocks: modes are read as components only. Both are held for a second set if a kind stays unheard.

8. **How a sent row stands to what was planted** (in the harness, which alone knows the truth): *full*, it names the link's series and no other (for many weak series, a gathered term with at least half its weight on them); *part*, some of them, or them and others; *copy*, a series it names is a near copy of a planted input (ranks go together at 0.5 or more over the whole table, the measure of Stage A1); *path*, its outcome and series share a slow path; *other*, none of these. A pass of a copy predicts and is not a false pass; a pass of *other* is.

Shared slow paths, seven shapes, six worlds each, with the floor: at 240 places 2 of 42 worlds had a pass, both of the shape with regimes, each one row (a low tail in the series to a high event in the outcome, lifts 3.3 and 3.22), and both passes carried the label; at 120 places none of 42 had a pass. Rows sent in path worlds 0 to 7.

The last smoke runs at 480 places (threshold, extremes, many weak and hidden mover, and fourteen path worlds) finished, and their outputs were lost unread when the scratch directory was cleared overnight; they were not run again. The other numbers in this section are from this session's record of outputs that went the same way.

In linked worlds with the floor, no pass named nothing planted; the five flagged at 120 places were near copies of a planted input.

## What is not known

- The false-pass rate with few rows sent, on worlds not used for shaping. That is the count's first and second marks.
- Whether the swarm proposes the shared slow paths that the label must then catch; on smoke worlds it rarely sends one.
- Hidden movers at 120 places (8 of 12) and links from a series common to all places are heard less; a sign that turns on a state is barely heard by the board or by its own answer.
- Lags beyond two years, and links acting within the year, are not proposed by any member.
