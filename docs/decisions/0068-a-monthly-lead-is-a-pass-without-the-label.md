# 0068: A monthly lead is a pass without the "together" label, to be counted before it is used

Date: 2026-11-12. Status: accepted (this session, under decision 0040).

## Context

Two reads of the yearly panel of four sets are silent (`results/stage-b-first-read.md`, `results/stage-c-second-read.md`). The second could hear only about three strong planted links in ten. More of the separate data sets (events, prices, displacement) are monthly, and the monthly read hears far smaller links on made-up tables, but its first count missed a mark: it takes a same-month tie for a one-month lead about half the time (`results/monthly-count.md`). After that count it was seen that the label of decision 0061 was on all 35 false passes and on 24 of 870 true ones. That was seen after the numbers.

## Decision

Count it afresh on unused seeds, marks first (`results/monthly-lead-count.md`): a lead is a pass whose label does not say "together"; a labelled pass is reported as moving together and never as a lead. Two panels carry late starts and gaps, which the first count lacked. Nothing in the read, the referee or the label's thresholds is changed.

## Consequences

If every mark holds, a rule for a real monthly read can be written. If the tie mark is missed again, monthly passes are reported only as moving together. The choice to make the label part of what is called a lead was suggested by counted rows; that is why it is counted on its own before any real series is read with it.
