# 0053: What the independent check changed in how the harvest result is stated

Status: accepted, 2026-11-10. Made by this session under decision 0040; reported to the owner, his to overrule. Changes the words of the result in `results/harvest-drought.md` and `README.md`. No change raises a number or widens a claim; the verdict and every registered number stand.

## Context

The rule said a pass "gets an independent reader before it is called a finding". The real record was scored once and passed (year-end 1.67%, mid-year 1.11%). A reader with no part in the work then checked the result under a brief committed before it reported. Its report is `docs/reviews/2026-11-10-harvest-result-check/report.md`; its verdict, "stands with changes".

It rebuilt the forecast with its own code (1.2%, all 62 other-year controls beaten), found no leak, and showed the gain is each country's own weather and not a world-wide year. It also showed that the first write-up said more than the numbers support, in six places.

## Decision

1. **The size is stated as a share of squared error, with its plain size beside it** (about 0.8% of a typical error year-end, about 0.6% mid-year). The rule always defined S on squared error; the first plain-language sentences dropped the word. The size words (under 1%, 1% to 5%, 5% or more) were registered on S and are still applied to S.
2. **A second interval is reported beside the registered one.** The registered interval resamples years only (0.97% to 2.35%). With countries and years both resampled it is 0.6% to 2.8% (mid-year 0.2% to 2.0%). The registered interval is the one the verdict used and is not replaced; the wider one is what a reader should take as the honest range.
3. **The sentence that turned the score into a link strength is withdrawn.** The real size exceeds the made-up ruler's strongest case, and nothing more follows.
4. **Who carries it is said:** ten countries give 55% of the gain, twenty give 85%, and without the 28 that gain most the rest sum to nothing.
5. **The other half of the chosen-third reading is said:** the remaining two-thirds of ranked records gained nothing after 2005 (−0.21%).
6. **The sentence on autumn cereals is narrowed** to maize and millet, and the README's count of no-link readings is given as 70 records read two ways.
7. **With the check done, the result is called a finding,** as the rule provided: a known link, small, carried by about a fifth of the countries, not proof of cause.

## Consequences

- The project session reproduced the reader's main numbers with the project's own run before acting (the plain size, the country shares, the two-way interval, the three figures for the chosen third). All agree.
- Nothing was re-scored. The reader's runs and the reproduction are checks on a record already spent for this driver, and no number from them replaces a registered one.
- An interval that resamples only years is too narrow wherever units share a country. The next rule registers the two-way interval from the start.
- The next test, whether the gain is large enough to use where it has helped before, needs records not yet read; the chosen-third figure on this record is a description, not a confirmation.
