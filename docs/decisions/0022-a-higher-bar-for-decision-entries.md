# 0022: A higher bar for decision entries

**Date:** 2026-11-05, evening. **Status:** accepted. The owner's word, typed in this session.

## Context

The owner asked: "Why are we keeping historical decisions?" Twenty-one entries had built up in one day, many of them for small choices. The answer given was that they are worth keeping as a record, because they let an outsider check that no rule moved after a number was seen, but that they should not be something a session has to read. The proposal was to raise the bar for new entries.

## Decision

His words: "yes raise the bar, write it into the rules".

A new entry is written only for:

- a choice that could favour a result;
- a change to a rule;
- anything the owner ruled on.

Any other choice goes in its commit message or the results file. `STATUS.md`, `CLAUDE.md` and the plan say what is true now. A decision is opened only when its reason is needed.

## Consequences

Existing entries stay as they are, immutable. The brief still sets the form of an entry and asks for its own decisions to be recorded; it sets no bar on what else gets one, so the two do not disagree.

When in doubt about whether a choice could favour a result, it gets an entry: that is the case the record exists for.
